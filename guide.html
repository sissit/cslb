谁需要执照

在加州承接工料合计 $1,000 及以上的工程，就要有 CSLB 执照（2025 年起由 $500 提高到 $1,000）。B 类（General Building）适用于涉及两个以上不相关工种的房建项目；A 类是土木和基建工程，B-2 是住宅改造，C 类是各专业分包。

加州不认德州等州的执照，也没有和德州的互惠协议。外州公司要在加州持证，需先在加州州务卿登记，再由一位合格的 qualifier 担保申请。

申请步骤

  1. 确定持证主体和 qualifier。LLC 在加州州务卿登记（外州 LLC 用 LLC-5 表）；qualifier 要有近 10 年内至少 4 年熟练级经验。
  2. 递交申请。Application for Original Contractor License 邮寄到 CSLB，附 $450 申请费和工作经验证明。一次只能申请一个类别；保证金和首发费不要一起交。
  3. 等确认信。4–6 周内收到，含申请费编号和 PIN，可在 CSLB 官网查进度。
  4. 指纹和审核。所有登记人员做 Live Scan 指纹背景调查，CSLB 核实经验。
  5. 考试。qualifier 考法律与商务和 B 类专业两门；符合条件可免考。
  6. 发证。收到通知后提交保证金、保险证明和首发费，完成石棉开卷考试，等待发证。
提速要点：表格一次填对；收到确认信马上约指纹；提前拿保证金和保险报价；每周看 CSLB 官网的 Processing Times 页面。

如何免考

只有 qualifier 一个人要考试。qualifier 满足下面任一条，就不用考：

  • 目前是一张同类别、状态良好的加州执照的 qualifier
  • 过去 5 年内当过同类别、状态良好的加州执照的 qualifier
  • 过去 5 年内在同一类别通过了两门考试

另有针对家族企业的豁免（Waiver），由 CSLB 酌情批准。亚利桑那、内华达、路易斯安那、密西西比、北卡等州的互惠只免专业考试，法律与商务照样要考。

外聘 RME：资格、要求与费用

  • 资格：有效的加州 B 类执照，或近 5 年内当过 B 类 qualifier；执照无处分；同时只能给一家公司当 RME。
  • 要求：W-2 全职员工，每周 ≥ 32 小时或营业时间的 80%，对施工进行直接监督和控制。
  • 费用：按施工经理水平，加州年薪约 $10–16 万（估计），另加福利和雇主税约 20–30%，以及 $25k qualifier 保证金。
网上常见每月几百到一千多美元的远程“挂证”。人不在岗、不管施工，违反直接监督要求，执照可能被吊销，公司和担保人都要担责，不建议。

考试

法律与商务和 B 类专业两门，四选一单选，闭卷，PSI 考场电脑作答，当场出成绩。申请受理后有 18 个月考过，次数不限，两次之间至少隔 3 周，改约每次 $60。考试有英文和西班牙语版本，没有中文版。各部分占比和考点见 知识点讲义，练习题见 章节练习。

California B license guide

With a complete application and passing scores on the first try, expect about 3–6 months from filing to license. State fees are about $1,000; with bonds and insurance, the first year usually runs $5,000–10,000 or more.

Who needs a license

Any California job of $1,000 or more in labor and materials requires a CSLB license (raised from $500 in 2025). The B (General Building) class covers building projects involving two or more unrelated trades; A is general engineering, B-2 is residential remodeling, and C classes are specialty trades.

California does not accept Texas licenses and has no reciprocity with Texas. An out-of-state company must first register with the California Secretary of State and then apply with a qualifying individual.

Application steps

  1. Set up the entity and qualifier. Register the LLC with the California Secretary of State (form LLC-5 for an out-of-state LLC). The qualifier needs at least 4 years of journey-level experience within the last 10.
  2. File the application. Mail the Application for Original Contractor License with the $450 fee and the experience certification. One class at a time; don't send bonds or the license fee yet.
  3. Acknowledgment. Within 4–6 weeks you receive an application fee number and PIN to track status online.
  4. Fingerprints and review. Everyone listed on the application completes Live Scan; CSLB verifies experience.
  5. Exams. The qualifier takes Law & Business and the B trade exam, unless exempt.
  6. Issuance. Submit bonds, proof of insurance and the license fee, complete the open-book asbestos exam, and the license issues.
To save time: get the forms right the first time, book Live Scan as soon as the acknowledgment arrives, get bond and insurance quotes early, and check CSLB's Processing Times page weekly.

LLC checklist

With the application

  • Secretary of State registration and Statement of Information; federal EIN
  • LLC application listing all personnel of record
  • Qualifier details and Certification of Work Experience signed by employers, coworkers or clients
  • For the no-exam route, the qualifier's California license history
  • $450 application fee

Before issuance

  • $25,000 contractor bond; another $25,000 bond of qualifying individual with an RME
  • $100,000 LLC employee/worker bond
  • Liability insurance: at least $1M for 5 or fewer personnel, plus $100k each additional, up to $5M
  • Proof of workers' compensation; with no employees you may file an exemption (except C-8, C-20, C-22, C-39, D-49). From January 1, 2028 every contractor must carry it
  • $350 initial license fee (non-sole owner)

Exam waiver

Only the qualifier takes the exams. No exam is required if the qualifier:

  • currently qualifies a California license in good standing in the same class;
  • qualified one in the same class within the past five years; or
  • passed both exams in the same class within the past five years.

CSLB may also grant a discretionary waiver for family businesses. Reciprocity with Arizona, Nevada, Louisiana, Mississippi and North Carolina waives only the trade exam; Law & Business is still required.

Hiring an RME

  • Qualifications: an active California B license, or B qualifier experience within 5 years; no discipline; may be RME for only one company.
  • Duties: a W-2 employee working ≥ 32 hours a week or 80% of business hours, exercising direct supervision and control.
  • Cost: roughly $100,000–160,000 a year at construction-manager level (estimate), plus 20–30% for benefits and payroll taxes, and the $25k qualifier bond.
Remote "license rental" offers of a few hundred to over a thousand dollars a month are common online. A qualifier who isn't present and doesn't supervise violates the direct-supervision rule; the license can be revoked and both the company and the qualifier are liable.

The exams

Law & Business and General Building (B): four-choice, closed-book, computer-based at PSI, results on the spot. After the application is accepted you have 18 months to pass, with unlimited attempts at least 3 weeks apart and a $60 rescheduling fee. Exams are offered in English and Spanish.
